## About Us

All Building & Property Services is an established, professional remedial building services contractor. We provide high-quality service and reliable workmanship to residential, multi-residential, and commercial properties throughout Sydney. Our services cover a wide range of building and property maintenance tasks, ensuring the safety and compliance of your property.

We are fully insured and provide transparent pricing with no hidden costs. Our team is available for 24/7 emergency response to resolve any urgent building issues.

## Services

### 1. **Remedial Repairs & Maintenance**
   - Full remedial construction and defect rectification for apartments, balconies, rooftops, and structural concrete.
   - Concrete spalling and structural repairs using high-strength mortars and coatings.
   - Waterproofing membranes for balconies, roofs, and planter boxes.

### 2. **Leak Investigations & Waterproofing**
   - Moisture mapping, intrusive testing, and comprehensive reporting for leaks.
   - Waterproofing for residential, strata, and commercial buildings to prevent water damage.

### 3. **Fire Safety & Compliance**
   - Fire door repairs, fire-rated sealing, and penetration fire-stopping.
   - Installation of compliant door hardware and signage to meet fire order requirements.
   - Fire safety assessments and reporting to meet compliance standards.

### 4. **Renovations & Refurbishments**
   - Full renovations for apartments, bathrooms, kitchens, and common areas.
   - Project management, design, and scheduling for strata or tenancy refurbishments.

### 5. **Handyman Services**
   - General maintenance and repairs including door/window repairs, plasterboard patching, fly screen installation, and more.

### 6. **Plumbing & Electrical**
   - Licensed plumbing services including blocked drains, pipe relining, and hot water tank replacements.
   - Electrical services such as lighting upgrades, smoke alarm installations, and switchboard safety checks.

### 7. **Painting & Coating Services**
   - Interior and exterior painting services for residential, commercial, and strata properties.
   - Graffiti removal and anti-graffiti coating applications.
